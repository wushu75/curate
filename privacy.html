Privacy policy

Strictly off the record.

An editor's notes are confidential by tradition. Curate keeps them that way by never having them to begin with.

Effective 1 October 2026

Curate stores everything locally in your browser. It makes no network requests, has no accounts, collects no analytics, and sends no telemetry. There is nothing to leak because there is nothing to collect.

What Curate stores

All data is kept in chrome.storage.local inside your browser profile on your device. This includes:

  • Private notes you write about submissions
  • Decline and feedback templates you create or customise
  • Your settings (editor name, publication name, insert preferences)
  • Template usage counts (e.g. "Used 3x") for your own reference

Nothing is sent to any server. Nothing is stored in the cloud. Nothing leaves your browser.

What Curate does not collect

  • No personal information or identifiers
  • No browsing history or page content
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reports
  • No cookies, fingerprinting or tracking pixels
  • No usage data of any kind sent anywhere

Network requests

Curate makes zero network requests. The only fetch() call in the entire extension loads Curate's own bundled CSS file from inside the extension package. No data is ever transmitted to any external server, including our own.

Permissions

Curate requests exactly two permissions:

  • storage — to save your notes, templates and settings locally in your browser
  • Host access to medium.com and *.medium.com — to display the Curate panel on Medium pages and detect submission fields

Curate does not run on any other website. It does not request access to your tabs, browsing history, downloads or any other browser capability.

Page interaction

Curate reads limited information from the Medium page to provide its features:

  • The submission ID (from the URL or page metadata) to link your note to the right story
  • The writer's name and story title (from visible page text) to fill template placeholders
  • The presence of Medium's note/decline input fields, so the Insert button knows where to place text

Curate never modifies the story content itself. It never reads or stores the body text of any submission. When you click Insert, Curate fills only the specific Medium input field you have open — exactly as if you had typed the text yourself.

Isolation

Curate's side panel runs inside a closed Shadow DOM. This means:

  • Medium's page scripts cannot read what you type into Curate
  • Keystrokes entered in the Curate panel are prevented from reaching Medium's keyboard handlers
  • Curate's styles never interfere with Medium's interface

Data syncing

Curate deliberately uses chrome.storage.local instead of chrome.storage.sync. This means your notes do not sync between computers through your Google account. This is a privacy choice: your editorial notes should not travel through any third-party account.

To move data between machines, use Curate's Export and Import feature, which saves and loads a JSON file directly on your device.

Data deletion

You are in full control of your data at all times:

  • Delete individual notes from the Curate panel
  • Clear all notes at once from the Manage Templates page
  • Delete individual templates or reset to defaults
  • Uninstall the extension to erase everything permanently

Third parties

Curate has no third-party dependencies, no third-party scripts, no third-party services. The extension is built with plain vanilla JavaScript, has zero npm dependencies, and loads no external code at runtime. There is no third party to share data with because there is no third party involved.

Children's privacy

Curate does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no data to protect in this regard.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Since Curate collects nothing, any future change would be a reduction in what little the extension does, not an expansion.

Contact
