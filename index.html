The editorial process, in three moves

01 — Read

Open the submission

A small button waits in the corner of medium.com. Curate recognises the story you're reviewing from its ID — on drafts, stories and your publication's inbox.

02 — Annotate

Think out loud, privately

Jot your thoughts in the side panel. They save as you type and come back whenever you reopen that submission. Press Alt+Shift+C from anywhere on Medium.

03 — Respond

Insert, review, send

Open Medium's note field and click Insert. The letter drops in, personalised. No field open? It's copied, ready to paste.

Privacy policy, in full

Strictly off the record.

An editor's notes are confidential by tradition. Curate keeps them that way by never having them to begin with.

  • ✓
    Stored in your browser only
    Notes, templates and settings live in chrome.storage.local on this device.
  • ✓
    No network requests
    No servers, no analytics, no remote code. Nothing to breach.
  • ✓
    Sealed off from the page
    The panel runs in a closed Shadow DOM that Medium's scripts can't read.
  • ✓
    Two permissions, nothing more
    Storage, and access to medium.com. It doesn't run anywhere else.
  • ✓
    Yours to export or erase
    Back up as JSON, share templates with co-editors, or delete everything in a click.

Letters to the editor

Does Medium see my notes?

No. Notes never leave your browser. Only the template text you choose to insert is placed into Medium's field — exactly as if you had typed it yourself.

Do notes sync between my computers?

Not in this version, deliberately: syncing would route your notes through an account. Use Export and Import to move them between machines.

What happens when Medium changes its design?

Notes and templates keep working. If Curate can't find Medium's field, Insert falls back to copying the letter so you can paste it.

Is Curate made by Medium?

No. Curate is an independent tool built for publication editors and is not affiliated with Medium.
